My First Romance Book

Twilight

Stephenie Meyer

★★★★½

My first romance read — a slow-burn love story I really enjoyed, even while questioning some of Bella's choices.

Romance Fantasy Young Adult Slow Burn

My Biggest Takeaway

I loved the romance, but I still wanted Bella to choose herself a little more.

Twilight made me enjoy the sweetness of a slow-burn romance, while also making me constantly question Bella's dependence on Edward. Cute? Absolutely. But girl, you can be independent too.

Twilight is the first romance book I read. The main character is Bella (Isabella), and personally, I felt her character was very weak and cringe at times. However, she is the one who narrates the story to us.

I felt it would have been better if Bella had been depicted as a stronger girl. But then again, maybe it is because she is a teenager and is intentionally shown that way.

But I really loved the romance part of the book. It was a slow-burn romance and very enjoyable. The twists were unpredictable and quite intriguing too.

The best part for me is Edward. At the beginning of the story, I was quite annoyed by Edward making Bella uncomfortable and following her around.

But as the story unfolded, it seemed like Bella really needed this guy to take care of her. So if you overlook my constant “Girl, can't you be independent?” thoughts, there were some really cute moments here and there.

Overall, I liked the book. My only major complaint is that I wish Bella had been written as a little stronger.
