My Full Review

What I Wrote After Finishing It

My original thoughts, rewritten from notebook to screen.

Sherlock Holmes was the first book that I actually bought specifically to read. At first, I doubted whether I would be able to complete it, but as I continued, I started really enjoying the language and the way the stories were written.

One thing I especially liked was the old British style of expression. I still remember phrases such as “Pray proceed”. That kind of wording made the book feel completely different from the modern stories I was used to reading.

The book is made up of shorter stories, and in each one Sherlock observes details, identifies the person connected to the crime, and works through the situation using logic and deduction.

Out of all the stories, one stood especially close to my heart. Sherlock finds the person responsible for the crime, but instead of reporting them immediately, he chooses mercy. I liked that moment because it showed that Sherlock was not simply a machine solving problems — there was also judgement and humanity behind his decisions.

Overall, I found Sherlock Holmes very interesting to read. I would recommend it to someone who enjoys problem solving, mystery, observation, and trying to understand how small details can reveal something much bigger.

And after finishing it, I genuinely felt like I had started behaving a little like Sherlock myself — looking around the house and trying to notice hidden clues everywhere.
