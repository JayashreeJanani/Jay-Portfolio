My First Book

The Secrets We Share

Emma Hannigan

★★★★★

The book that started my reading journey. A story of relationships, secrets, forgiveness, and family — and the beginning of a hobby I didn't expect to fall in love with.

Family Relationships Drama Emotional

The Secrets We Share was the first book I ever completed. I borrowed it from a public library, and at first I was quite unsure whether I would actually finish it. However, as I kept reading, I found the book increasingly intriguing.

From the beginning, the story unfolds almost like a cinematic opening. Something from the past has separated a mother and son, immediately creating questions about what happened and why. Without giving away spoilers, I really loved how the author turned the story into a page-turner.

In terms of genre, the book sits somewhere around contemporary fiction, women's fiction, family drama, and romance. Personally, though, I wouldn't describe it mainly as a romance. There are romantic moments, but they never felt like the heart of the story to me.

One thing that particularly stood out was the way Emma Hannigan kept making the story more interesting as it progressed. At times, reading it almost felt like waiting for the next episode of a series — I wanted to know what would happen next.

For me, the story became a blend of trust, betrayal, forgiveness, and most importantly, family. Before I really realised it, I had reached the end.

After finishing the final page, I actually had a little book hangover. That was probably the moment I realised how much the story had pulled me into its world.

Most importantly, I liked the way the author seemed to bring aspects of her own life journey and emotions into the story. That personal quality made parts of the book feel more genuine and memorable to me.
