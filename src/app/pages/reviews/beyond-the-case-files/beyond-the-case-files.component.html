Book 08

Beyond the Case File

Alan J. Parapilly

Four recovery journeys that changed the way I looked at social work — and showed me the patience, strength, and kindness behind the profession.

Social Work Real Stories Recovery Humanity

My Biggest Takeaway

Social work is something that keeps kindness alive.

Before reading this book, I thought social work was just an easy job. But these stories showed me how much patience, emotional strength, and kindness it can take to help people through some of the most challenging moments of their lives.

Beyond the Case File is written by my friend, and this is his first book. So before getting into my review, hearty congratulations, my friend!

The book revolves around the life of a social worker and documents the experiences of people he encountered through his work.

Before reading this book, I thought social work was just an easy job. However, this book made me realise that a social worker's life has to go through many twists and turns while handling people.

These people are trained in their job, and yet they might encounter something more challenging than what they are used to. Even in those challenging times, I still wonder how they are able to remain so calm while handling people.

This book revolves around four people's recovery journeys. Some are pretty inspirational, while some brought out a deep sense of sympathy in me.

Spoiler alert!! One should definitely not miss the last story because that was genuinely different from the others. I couldn't even predict that this man's journey might end up like that.

That was something I liked the most, and the journey towards the end became more emotional and heartfelt.

Overall, I recommend this book to everyone because social work is something that keeps kindness alive. And I saw abundant kindness in this book through the eyes of a social worker.
